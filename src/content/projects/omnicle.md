## Overview

Omnicle is an enterprise AI automation platform built around one idea: automation that thinks ahead. It turns an organisation's knowledge into intelligent workflows and interfaces that automate routine tasks, connect systems, answer questions, and keep adapting as they learn.

## The problem

Organisations of 100+ people are full of coordination-heavy, repetitive, high-stakes work — clustering support tickets, pulling reports together from a dozen sources, comparing vendor proposals line by line. That work eats skilled people's time and is exactly the kind of process that's too nuanced for rigid, rule-based automation.

## What it does

- **Conversational intelligence** — turns institutional knowledge into workflows and interfaces that automate tasks, connect systems and answer questions, adapting as they learn.
- **Workflow design** — predefined task sequences triggered by user actions or system events, with interaction patterns that adapt to the input.
- **System integration** — connects to email, calendars, Teams, Drive, CRM systems and databases to unify otherwise disconnected data.
- **Proactive automation** — monitors incoming data and fires workflows automatically when defined conditions emerge, rather than waiting to be asked.

## Where it fits

- Spotting emerging patterns in high-volume data, e.g. clustering related support tickets
- Automating report generation that would otherwise be assembled by hand from multiple sources
- Comparing complex vendor proposals and extracting standardised information
- Building an institutional knowledge base that stays accessible across the organisation

## Delivery

Built on Azure cloud infrastructure and delivered as a working proof-of-concept within two weeks, with no upfront cost — data collection, workflow templating, trigger configuration and output visualisation set up against a real process before any commitment.
